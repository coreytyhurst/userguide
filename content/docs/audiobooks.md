# Audiobooks

Listen to audiobooks and podcasts from the Farmhouse server.

**Open the app:** [books.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://books.tyhurstfarmhouse.com)

## On your computer

1. Go to [books.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://books.tyhurstfarmhouse.com).
2. Log in with your Farmhouse credentials.
3. Browse the library or search for a specific title.
4. Click a book and hit play. The web player tracks your progress automatically.

## On your phone or tablet

Download the **Audiobookshelf** app for the best experience:

- **iPhone/iPad:** [Audiobookshelf on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/audiobookshelf-app/id6475221402)
- **Android:** [Audiobookshelf on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.audiobookshelf.app)

When setting up the app:
1. Set the server address to `https://books.tyhurstfarmhouse.com`
2. Log in with your Farmhouse credentials.

## Good to know

- Your listening progress syncs across all devices.
- You can set a sleep timer for bedtime listening.
- The audiobook library is smaller than the movie/music libraries. If you're looking for a specific title, let Corey know and he can try to add it.
- Unlike the streaming and music apps, audiobook content is added manually, so it may take a bit longer for new titles to appear.
