# Getting Started

Welcome! The Farmhouse server hosts a bunch of apps for watching movies, listening to music, playing games, and more. Everything runs from our home server and is available to family and friends.

## What you need

- **An account.** You should have received a username and password from Corey. If you haven't, just ask.
- **A device.** Any phone, tablet, computer, or smart TV will work for most apps.
- **An internet connection.** Everything works over the internet, whether you're at the farmhouse or anywhere else.

## Quick start

1. **Go to the dashboard** at [home.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://home.tyhurstfarmhouse.com) to see all available apps in one place.
2. **Pick an app** from the dashboard or from this guide.
3. **Log in** with the username and password you were given.

> Most apps share the same login, but some (like the music app) have their own separate accounts. Each guide page will tell you which login to use.

## Tips

- **Use Chrome or Edge** for the best experience on a computer. Safari works but occasionally has quirks.
- **Bookmark the dashboard** so you can always find your way back.
- **Check this guide** if something isn't working before reaching out. The [Troubleshooting]({{< relref "/docs/troubleshooting" >}}) page covers the most common fixes.
