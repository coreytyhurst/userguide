# Troubleshooting

Try these steps before reaching out. They fix most issues.

## The page won't load

1. **Check your internet connection.** Try loading a regular website like google.com.
2. **Try a different browser.** Chrome and Edge work best.
3. **Clear your browser cache.** This fixes a surprising number of issues.
   - Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Clear browsing data
   - Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Clear browsing data
4. **Wait a few minutes and try again.** The server may be restarting after an update.

## Video is buffering or choppy

1. **Lower the video quality.** In the streaming app, click the gear icon during playback and choose a lower quality setting.
2. **Try a wired connection** instead of Wi-Fi if possible.
3. **Close other tabs and apps** that might be using bandwidth.
4. **Try a different browser.** Chrome generally handles video best.

## The app says "unable to connect to server"

This usually means the server is temporarily down for maintenance or updates. Wait 10-15 minutes and try again. If it's still down after an hour, let Corey know.

## Music app won't connect

1. **Double-check the server address:** `https://music.tyhurstfarmhouse.com`
2. **Make sure you're using your music account** (not your streaming login -- they're separate).
3. **Try logging in through the web player first** to confirm your credentials work.

## I forgot my password

Ask Corey to reset it for you.

## Something else?

If none of the above helps, let Corey know with as much detail as you can:
- What app were you using?
- What were you trying to do?
- What happened instead?
- What device and browser are you on?

The more specifics, the faster the fix.
