# Retro Gaming

Play classic retro games right in your browser. No downloads or emulators needed.

**Open the app:** [gaming.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://gaming.tyhurstfarmhouse.com)

## How to play

1. Go to [gaming.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://gaming.tyhurstfarmhouse.com).
2. Browse the library of games by system (NES, SNES, Game Boy, Sega Genesis, and more).
3. Click a game to start playing.

Everything runs in your browser. A keyboard works for most games, but you can also connect a USB or Bluetooth controller for the full experience.

## Controller support

Most modern controllers work when plugged in or connected via Bluetooth:

- Xbox controllers
- PlayStation controllers
- Nintendo Switch Pro controllers
- Generic USB gamepads

Your browser should detect the controller automatically. If it doesn't, try refreshing the page after connecting.

## Good to know

- **Best on a computer.** The games work on phones and tablets, but a bigger screen and a controller are much more fun.
- **Use Chrome or Edge** for the best compatibility.
- Game saves work within your browser session. Clearing your browser data will reset your progress.
