# Dungeons & Dragons

The Farmhouse hosts a virtual tabletop for D&D sessions using FoundryVTT.

**Open the app:** [dnd1.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://dnd1.tyhurstfarmhouse.com)

## Joining a session

1. Open [dnd1.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://dnd1.tyhurstfarmhouse.com) in **Chrome or Edge** (required for best compatibility).
2. Select your game world from the list.
3. Log in with the player credentials your DM gave you.
4. Your character sheet and the game map will load automatically.

## Requirements

- **Browser:** Chrome or Edge. Firefox works but may have performance issues. Safari is not recommended.
- **Screen size:** A laptop or desktop monitor is strongly recommended. Tablets can work in a pinch but the interface is designed for larger screens.
- **Internet connection:** A stable connection is important since the game runs in real-time.

## Good to know

- Your DM manages character sheets, maps, and encounters. If you need something changed on your sheet, ask your DM.
- The [Baklor's House](https://baklor.tyhurstfarmhouse.com) site has lore, world info, and campaign resources for our D&D group.
- If the game world isn't loading, check with your DM first. Sessions may not always be running.
