# Projects

A few other things Corey is involved in. None of this is required reading — it's here in
case you find it interesting.

## Baklor's House

**Visit:** [baklor.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://baklor.tyhurstfarmhouse.com)

A companion site for the Farmhouse D&D group -- lore, maps, world history, and campaign
resources. If you play in a campaign it's where the world is documented, and even if you
don't, it's a fun look at what the group has built.

## Ruthie -- the dinner decider

**Visit:** [ruthie.huey-holdings.com](https://ruthie.huey-holdings.com)

Can't decide what's for dinner? Ruthie picks for you. She suggests a meal for tonight --
a home-cooked dish or a restaurant -- and learns from your family's feedback to make
better suggestions over time.
