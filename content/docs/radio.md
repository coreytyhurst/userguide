# Radio

The Farmhouse runs its own internet radio stations -- continuous music you can listen to in any browser. No login required. Each station plays a different style, 24/7.

> [!NOTE]
> Open a station using the links below. The bare `radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com` address is the admin page, not a player -- it won't play music.

## The stations

### Farmhouse Radio

NSFW or Kids. This is all of the music available on the farmhouse server!

**Listen:** [radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com/public/farmhouse-radio](https://radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com/public/farmhouse-radio)

### Freedom Radio

Only bangers! A party playlist radio station for when you are drinking beers and hanging out with buds!

**Listen:** [radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com/public/freedom-radio](https://radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com/public/freedom-radio)

### Sonic Temple

All of the bands on the Sunday of Sonic Temple 2026 in Columbus.

**Listen:** [radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com/public/sonic_temple](https://radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com/public/sonic_temple)

## How to listen

- **In your browser:** open any station link above and it starts playing.
- **On your phone:** open the link in your mobile browser, or paste the station's stream URL into any internet radio app. The stream URL is shown on each station's public page.

## Good to know

- No account needed -- anyone with a link can listen.
- The stations run 24/7 automatically (powered by AzuraCast).
- Song requests and playlist feedback are always welcome -- just let Corey know.
