# Freedom Radio

Listen to Freedom Radio, the Farmhouse's own internet radio station. It plays a mix of music 24/7, curated from the Farmhouse music library.

**Listen now:** [radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com)

## How to listen

### In your browser

Just open [radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://radio.tyhurstfarmhouse.com) and the station starts playing. No login required.

### On your phone

You can listen in your mobile browser, or use any internet radio app that supports custom stream URLs. The direct stream URL is available on the radio page.

## What's playing

The station plays a rotating mix of music from the Farmhouse library. The schedule and current track info are shown on the radio page.

## Good to know

- No account needed. Anyone with the link can listen.
- The station runs 24/7 automatically.
- Song requests and playlist feedback are always welcome. Just let Corey know.
