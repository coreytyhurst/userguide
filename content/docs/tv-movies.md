# TV & Movies

Stream TV shows and movies from the Farmhouse server, similar to Netflix or Hulu but with our own library.

**Open the app:** [stream.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://stream.tyhurstfarmhouse.com)

## How it works

The streaming app (Jellyfin) has a library of movies and TV shows. You can browse, search, and stream anything in the library. New content gets added regularly.

## On your computer

1. Go to [stream.tyhurstfarmhouse.com](https://stream.tyhurstfarmhouse.com) in Chrome or Edge.
2. Log in with your Farmhouse credentials.
3. Browse the library or use the search bar to find something specific.
4. Click a title and hit play.

## On your phone or tablet

Download the **Jellyfin** app for the best experience:

- **iPhone/iPad:** [Jellyfin on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jellyfin-mobile/id1480732557)
- **Android:** [Jellyfin on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.jellyfin.mobile)

When setting up the app:
1. Set the server address to `https://stream.tyhurstfarmhouse.com`
2. Log in with your Farmhouse credentials.

## On your TV

- **Apple TV:** Download the Jellyfin app from the App Store.
- **Android TV / Google TV / Fire Stick:** Download Jellyfin from the app store on your device.
- **Roku:** Search for "Jellyfin" in the Roku Channel Store.

Use the same server address and login as above.

## Good to know

- You can create watch lists and mark favorites.
- Playback remembers where you left off, even across devices.
- If a movie or show is missing, you can [request it]({{< relref "/docs/requesting-content" >}}).
